# Building My Way Through Computer Science

I didn't really have a perfect plan when I started coding. I just knew I liked creating things and wanted to understand how the technology behind them actually worked.

What started with simple HTML and JavaScript slowly turned into exploring React, Next.js, Node.js, databases, Java, DSA, AI, blockchain, and everything in between. Every new technology opened another door — and usually another set of errors to fix.

## Learning by Building

I've always learned better by building something rather than just watching tutorials.

Over time, I've worked on projects around **AI, sustainability, agriculture, blockchain, web development, and student technology**. Some started as small ideas, some became full projects, and some taught me more through their failures than their successes.

Working on projects and hackathons also changed how I look at development. It's not just about writing code that works. It's about taking an idea, breaking the problem down, figuring things out when you don't know the answer, and somehow getting everything to work before the deadline.

## Still Learning

I'm currently exploring **DSA, backend development, databases, full-stack development, and AI**, while continuing to experiment with new technologies and ideas.

I don't want to be someone who only knows how to use a particular framework. I want to understand the fundamentals well enough to build, debug, and solve problems independently.

There are still plenty of things I don't know.

And that's actually what makes the journey exciting.

## Why This Blog?

This space is where I'll share that journey — the projects I'm building, technologies I'm learning, lessons from hackathons, things that break, things that finally work, and the occasional thought that probably started at 2 AM.

No perfectly planned roadmap.

Just **learning, building, breaking, fixing, and repeating.**

And hopefully, looking back someday and realizing how far all those small steps took me. 🚀
